# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-27)

First release, for the Android companion Pebble Phone Connect 1.0.0.

- Favorites as cards with the contact photo, the next and previous ones loaded ahead
- Contacts by initial or by voice, by name or nickname, recent calls marked missed, incoming, outgoing or declined, and a full keypad usable by touch
- Call screen modeled on the system one, with photo, timer, mute, audio route and hang up
- Messages displayed like system notifications, browsed by buttons or touch, with Select opening the reply menu
- Quick launch opens directly on the call in progress
- Conversations with unread ones marked, and full messages, SMS and RCS alike, pictures included
- Background refresh of conversations and messages, keeping the current selection
- The latest missed call or text under the app name in the launcher, with its age
- Replies by dictation, by emoji or with quick replies written on the phone, and the conversation opened on the phone from the same menu
- Alphanumeric senders cannot be replied to; their conversation opens on the phone in one tap
- Sending shows the system progress and "Sent" animation
- Menu and message text sizes follow the watch setting or the sizes set on the phone
- Icons in the PebbleOS style, most of them from its own set
- Round watches laid out like the system: centred menus, and a call screen with the curved action bar
- English and French
